FabricLab rdma-basics packet analysis
=====================================

Captures were taken on hosta interface data0 (the isolated 10.10.10.0/24 data
plane), not on the 192.168.100.0/24 TCP control network. tcpdump captured all
frames on data0; the lines below are the UDP/4791 RoCEv2 frames.

The ICMP echo request/reply and occasional ARP/ICMPv6 frames in the pcaps are
capture-readiness/neighbour-discovery traffic. They are not part of the RDMA
operations. The ordinary TCP control channel uses ctrl0 (192.168.100.0/24) and
is absent from these data0 captures by construction.
